Welcome

This course teaches practical cybersecurity awareness for everyday digital activities. You will learn how to protect accounts, recognise common scams, use devices more safely, and make careful choices about personal information — at work, at school, and at home.

Learning objectives

By the end of this course, you should understand:

  • Basic cybersecurity concepts
  • Password and account protection
  • Phishing and scam recognition
  • Social engineering
  • Device and internet security
  • Basic data protection
  • Safe cybersecurity decision-making
